Demo: one cycle of LeonidaLoop Studio

Each step below shows real output captured from the app on 2026-11-18. Secrets and tokens are never printed, so none appear here. Channel IDs are public.

Español ↓
  1. Connect an account through the official OAuth flow. The owner runs the app, a browser window opens the platform's own consent page, and the app keeps only an encrypted token. Afterwards it confirms which channel was authorized: 
    > conectar_youtube.cmd verify --account youtube_es
youtube_es: authorized channel → {"channel_id": "UClO1tC-zysYM57tIMCDQDgg", "title": "LeonidaLoop ES", "handle": "@leonidaloopesp", "privacy": "public"}
> conectar_youtube.cmd verify --account youtube_en
youtube_en: authorized channel → {"channel_id": "UCqXjZSERsJvM4P4P23Edubg", "title": "LeonidaLoop En", "handle": "@leonidaloopeng", "privacy": "public"}
    Scope: youtube.readonly only. If the authorized channel ID does not match the confirmed one, the app stops (exit code 3).
  2. Find GTA 6 topics from public metadata. The app reads public feeds every 30 minutes and ranks topics by how fast they are growing. Last run:
    MetricValue
    Creators monitored (ES + EN)20
    GTA 6 items in the last 72 h4,035
    Videos downloaded0 (metadata only)
    Clips with verified commercial permission0 → all blocked
    Flagged as risky (leaks, “free” scams…)8
    These items only point to trends. For original videos, LeonidaLoop writes its own script and analysis.
  3. Produce the video locally. Each original Short is rendered on the owner's PC at 1080×1920 with an AI narration voice (Kokoro-82M, Apache-2.0) and subtitles checked against the reviewed script. Short 001, both versions:
    VersionLengthSubtitle accuracyLoudnessStatus
    ES36.4 s100 %−14.2 LUFSawaiting approval
    EN38.0 s100 %−14.2 LUFSawaiting approval
  4. Rights gate. Before rendering in real mode, every input file is hashed and checked against the rights register. A file without a recorded license stops production: 
    # recorded result: real-mode test job using a third-party clip with no permission on record
status: blocked_rights · reason: sin_permiso (no verified commercial permission)
monetizable: false
  5. Human approval. Finished videos stay in the queue as awaiting_approval. The owner watches them and decides. The app has no automatic publishing.
  6. TikTok (pending review). When the TikTok app is approved, the same flow will run with Login Kit: sign in on TikTok's page → read user.info.basic (display name, avatar, open ID) → confirm it is the LeonidaLoop ES or EN account → store the token encrypted on the PC. Uploads will need a separate approval and remain human-approved.

Demostración (español)

Datos reales del 2026-11-18, sin secretos ni tokens. El ciclo tiene seis pasos:

  1. Conexión de cuentas: OAuth oficial con permiso mínimo (youtube.readonly). La app verifica el canal autorizado: «LeonidaLoop ES» y «LeonidaLoop En».
  2. Investigación: analiza metadatos públicos de 20 creadores: 4035 elementos de GTA 6 en 72 h, 0 vídeos descargados y todos los clips bloqueados sin permiso comercial.
  3. Producción local: Short 001 ES (36,4 s) y EN (38,0 s), con subtítulos al 100 % y −14,2 LUFS.
  4. Control de derechos: se bloquea cualquier archivo sin licencia registrada.
  5. Aprobación humana: nada se publica de forma automática.
  6. TikTok (pendiente de revisión): Login Kit con user.info.basic para confirmar la cuenta. El token se guarda cifrado en el equipo.
